About me

Hi, I'm Madhu.

I'm a senior frontend architect in Hyderabad with more than 11 years of building web platforms and mobile apps that real people use every day, from truck drivers in container yards to finance teams moving money across borders.

I started in 2014 writing native Android and iOS apps for port operations. That turned into hybrid mobile with Cordova, then Ionic and Capacitor, then large Angular platforms. Most recently I designed the microfrontend platform behind a cross-border payments product, where four teams now ship on their own schedules.

Teams tell me I'm the person who asks "what happens when the network drops?" before anyone else does. I like mentoring, I like boring, reliable releases, and I've built an AI-assisted delivery pipeline that does the repetitive parts of a ticket while a human still checks every commit.

Tools like ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot and Claude Code have become a natural extension of my workflow. I use them to explore approaches, write tests and handle boilerplate faster, then review every line myself, so the speed goes up without the quality going down.

I also work across the stack with React and Node.js, plus Express and MongoDB, so I can own a feature from the API to the last pixel.

If any of that sounds like what your team needs, let's talk.

What sets me apart

Plenty of developers can build the screen. What I add is the part around it: the architecture that lets teams move, the numbers that prove it worked, and the problems I catch before they reach production.

Platform thinking

I design the shell, the shared libraries and the boundaries, so each team owns its module and nobody waits on a single release train. At Intermex that meant 5+ microfrontends deploying on their own.

Problems caught early

Duplicate API calls, flaky auth on SSR, the screen that breaks offline. I look for these in planning and code review, so they never become a production incident.

Measured, not guessed

I baseline first and report the result after: 40–60% fewer redundant API calls, 30–40% faster first load, 60% faster sign-in. If it isn't measured, I don't claim it.

Web and mobile in one head

I've shipped native, hybrid and web. When your product has both an Angular portal and an Ionic app, you get one person who understands both, and one shared design for state, auth and offline.

Questions clients ask
Do we need microfrontends?

Often not. If one team owns the app, a well-structured Nx monorepo with lazy loading is usually enough. Microfrontends pay off when several teams need to release on their own schedules. The audit tells you which fits.

Can you work inside our team and tools?

Yes. I work in your repo, your board (Jira or Azure DevOps) and your CI, follow your review process, and pair with your developers.

Can you upgrade an old Angular or Cordova app?

Yes. I've worked with every Angular version from 4 to 20 and moved hybrid apps from Cordova to Capacitor. Upgrades go in steps, behind tests, without a big-bang rewrite.

Do you work with React and Node too?

Yes. I've shipped React in production and work with Node.js, Express and MongoDB, so I can cover full MEAN-stack features. For back-end-heavy projects I'll tell you upfront where I'm strongest.

Do you build mobile apps too?

Yes. Ionic and Capacitor apps for Android and iOS, including offline-first sync, biometrics and store releases. Most of my career started there.

How do you price?
